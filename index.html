OUR STORY

Team Story: GDG On Campus CTU

From a small group of passionate students to one of the most active tech communities in the region.

2023

Our Beginning

Founded in 2023, GDG On Campus CTU started as a small group of passionate students determined to bring Google's developer community to Cebu Technological University. What began as a vision to promote technology education has grown into one of the most active student tech communities in the region.

2023

The Foundation

  • Chapter Founded with initial core team of organizers
  • First Workshops: Launched beginner-friendly programming sessions
  • Community Building: Started building relationships with local tech professionals
2024

Major Breakthrough

  • TechConnect Event: Our flagship event bringing together students and industry professionals
  • Membership Growth: Reached 200+ active members milestone
  • Industry Partnerships: Established connections with local tech companies
  • Recognition: Gained recognition from Google and the university community

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about GDG CTU.

General

What is GDG On Campus CTU?

GDG On Campus CTU is a student-led technology community at Cebu Technological University that empowers aspiring developers through workshops, hackathons, collaborative projects, and networking events powered by Google technologies.

Membership

How can I become a member?

You can become a member by signing up through our registration form. All students of Cebu Technological University are welcome to join! Just click the "Join Us" button above.

Activities

What activities does GDG CTU organize?

We organize workshops, hackathons, tech talks, bootcamps, study jams, and community meetups. All activities are designed to help students learn new technologies and build their skills.

Benefits

What are the benefits of joining?

Members get access to exclusive workshops, networking opportunities with industry professionals, project collaboration, Google resources, certificates, and a supportive community of tech enthusiasts.

General

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all! We welcome everyone regardless of skill level. Our activities are designed for beginners to advanced learners. We believe in learning together and helping each other grow.
